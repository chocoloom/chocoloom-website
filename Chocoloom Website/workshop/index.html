Step-by-Step Journey

What Happens in the 3-Hour Workshop?

Designed for complete beginners. No prior art or sculpture experience required!

00 – 20 Mins

Welcome, Chocolate Shots & Kit Unboxing

Arrive at Chocoloom Cafe, collect your personalized sculpting apron, unbox your clay kit, and receive your FREE Chocoloom Canvas Tote Bag at reception.

20 – 60 Mins

Clay Conditioning, Color Mixing & Cake Base Shaping

Learn how to blend pastel clay colors, condition air-dry polymer clay, and sculpt a sturdy miniature sponge cake foundation with crisp edges.

60 – 100 Mins

Faux Frosting Piping & Ruffle Piping Masterclass

Hold pastry piping nozzles to apply realistic whipped clay cream, Lambeth scallops, shell borders, and delicate drips over your cake model.

100 – 150 Mins

Sculpting Miniature Toy Charms & Personalization

Craft miniature clay strawberries, teddy bears, cherries, candles, and letter banners. Decorate your cake to make it 100% unique to you!

150 – 180 Mins

Gloss Glazing, Acrylic Dome Sealing & Certificate Ceremony

Apply protective gloss glaze, seal your finished clay toy cake inside its acrylic display dome, take photos at the photobooth, and take home your certificate and tote bag!
